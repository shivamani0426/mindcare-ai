01 / PLAY

From picture puzzles to gentle matching games, MindCare gives every player room to explore familiar ideas in their own time. No races, no pressure—just little victories.

Friendly reminders offer a familiar rhythm for meals, activities, and everyday tasks. Large controls, spoken cues, and a simplified patient view help make each next step clearer.
