Call family on WhatsApp

MindCare opens your loved one's WhatsApp chat. You start the voice call in WhatsApp — it won't ring them until you tap the phone icon there.

  1. 1Tap Open WhatsApp next to a trusted person below.
  2. 2When their chat opens, tap the 📞 phone icon inside WhatsApp.
  3. 3Their WhatsApp rings. They can answer without installing MindCare.

Both people need WhatsApp and internet access. WhatsApp calls use internet data. For an emergency, use the separate 112 phone-dialer button above.

Want to speak without WhatsApp voice calling? Each contact also has a Free MindCare voice call option. It creates a private link you can send to them. They must tap the link and Answer to connect; this does not ring a normal SIM number.
